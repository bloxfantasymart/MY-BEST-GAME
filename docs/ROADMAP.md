# Progress and roadmap

## Implemented development build
- Procedural plaza, six assigned plots, roll station, assembly pod, ATM and 12 starting stands, expandable to 24. Models are geometric placeholders with basic idle motion and mutation effects. Roll trays, lever motion, ATM pad, a collection effect, a help panel, server-only leaderboards, and optional configurable sound hooks are included.
- A six-step tutorial follows server-verified rolls, purchases, assembly, placement and collection; completing it grants a one-time $1,000 bonus. Its saved step is added to older profiles on first load. The client highlights a relevant station. Existing players can also complete the tutorial once.
- Cosmetic plot themes unlock at rebirth 0, 10, 25, 50 and 100; the selected theme persists. Avatar titles update at the same milestones in both places. These are simple procedural colors/materials and text, not final artwork or full particle auras.
- A developer admin menu is disabled by default and gated by configured Roblox user IDs on the server. It can inspect online player inventories, adjust cash/rebirth, grant a Standard part, kick and trigger a 120-second server-only event. Actions require confirmation in the UI, validate numeric input and write basic non-durable output logs. Offline administration, persistent bans, creature grants and cross-server controls remain unimplemented. See `docs/ADMIN_SETUP.md`.
- Client display animation and basic Fire/Sparkles effects are disabled beyond 100 studs, with streaming-aware checks for required display parts. This is a lightweight LOD, not a substitute for full device/streaming performance testing.
- Mythic, Godly and Secret offers can be announced to the current server, with a five-second server-wide throttle. Announcements describe offers, not purchases; global cross-server broadcast is not implemented.
- Server-generated rarity-weighted rolls and mutation rolls, four part types, 50 catalog rows, nine slot prices, purchase/assembly/placement/income. Remote actions are throttled and physical station actions check proximity.
- Mutation weights: Standard 90%, Shiny 7%, Golden 2.4%, Void 0.5%, Glitched 0.1%. Each part's base contribution is multiplied by its mutation multiplier (1, 1.5, 2.5, 4 or 6); four identical mutations, including Standard, double the result. The sum is multiplied by the highest rarity multiplier and 0.4. Ties choose Head, Chest, Arms, Legs order. Effects are basic built-in sparkles/fire and client-side rainbow color, not final artwork.
- The plaza fusion forge unlocks at rebirth 15. It consumes three distinct, unplaced, non-ascended creatures at level 10 or above whose four components and mutations match exactly, plus a fee of `max(10000, floor(base income * 100))`. The result starts at level 1 with 5x one input's base income and a cosmetic crown. Each ascended creature boosts occupied north/south/east/west neighboring stands by 20%; bonuses add and never wrap across rows. Base stand labels include these bonuses but not temporary events or VIP. Offline income includes adjacency at its normal 25% efficiency. Unlike the spec's proposed max level 10, this build caps levels at 1,000 and accepts level 10 or above. Rebirth will only sacrifice non-ascended creatures. Fusion is irreversible.
- Placed creatures gain one level per 10 seconds and can also be upgraded with cash from the backpack; +25% of base income per level. This interprets the unspecified EXP threshold as one EXP per level. Level cap 1,000 and creature cap 100 are safety choices. Offline EXP is not included.
- Up to 100 rebirths. Requirement is floor(1000 * (current rebirth + 1)^2.7). Rebirth 1 requires Dirt Boy; subsequent targets use the last catalog entry unlocked at the **current** tier, so the sacrifice can be obtained before rebirthing. Cash and roll slots reset, the specified creature is removed (including from a stand), and other inventory, stands and pending cash remain. No paid skips yet. A configured KeepSlots gamepass can retain slots.
- UTC-synchronized events: each 30-minute period starts with a five-minute Frenzy that doubles non-common tier weights. At each 15-minute boundary the disaster alternates deterministically among Eclipse (3 min), Sigma (2 min), and Ohio Storm (2 min). Eclipse discounts newly rolled offers 50%; Sigma triples stand income; Storm spawns collectible golden drops near each plot every 10 seconds, each giving its owner $50 times (rebirth + 1). Every Friday 17:00 UTC through Sunday 23:59:59 UTC, Weekend Frenzy multiplies non-common tier weights by 1.5 and income by 2. Frenzy and weekend luck factors multiply; Sigma and weekend income factors multiply. The client adds a simple event tint. This is a deterministic global schedule, not randomized disasters or cross-server messaging.
- Five per-session gifts are implemented at 1, 5, 10, 15 and 40 minutes: $500, a random Uncommon part, $15,000, a 10-minute 2x luck boost, and $100,000. Claims are server-validated and reset on departure, as does the session timer; the luck expiration is saved and continues across places and offline time. Uncommon gifts can grant a part above the player's current unlock tier. The remaining requested gifts, crates, cosmetics and roll tokens are not implemented. Rejoining can earn session gifts again, which needs economy balancing before release.
- Unpurchased roll offers are discarded after 60 seconds; the server rejects late buys even if a client display is stale. The limited-time luck boost multiplies non-common rarity weights and stacks with weekend, Frenzy and pass weights, not necessarily the final rarity probabilities.
- Three fixed daily objectives track rolled parts, Rare+ assemblies and collected bank cash, with cash rewards and one claim each per UTC day. The seven-day consecutive-login calendar currently grants cash-only substitute rewards ($1,000, $2,500, $5,000, $50,000, $10,000, $250,000, $100,000); cosmetics, potions, boxes and the season pass are not yet implemented. Missing a UTC day resets the streak.
- On a successful main-place departure save, the logout time is recorded. Main-place load atomically adds 25% of placed-creature income including adjacency bonuses for up to 24 hours to pending cash, then clears the timestamp while acquiring the lease. The hub retains the timestamp, so time in the hub counts as away. Event and VIP multipliers, offline EXP and a dedicated welcome-back modal are not implemented. A crash or failed departure save may prevent offline earnings.
- Persistence uses a DataStore profile with an UpdateAsync lease, 45-second renewal/autosave, retries and shutdown flush. Studio defaults to memory-only; see README. It is a first implementation, not a proven production persistence library. A server that cannot renew for 120 seconds stops gameplay by kicking the player; the 180-second lease eventually expires after a crash. A failed final save may lose progress. New data starts at $200; the part price is max($10, base income * 5), discounted in Eclipse. These are prototype choices.
- Auto-roll unlocks at rebirth 5 or through the optional configured AutoRoll pass: every two seconds it rolls all slots and buys drops at or above a chosen rarity while near the station. It stops on insufficient cash or part capacity. Inventory supports filtered viewing, confirmed sales and rarity-based bulk sales. Bulk sales validate a fixed list of selected item IDs, including mutations, before removing anything. Part resale pays 50% of its original offer price; creature resale pays 50% of the four parts’ original prices (to prevent crafting/reselling from creating cash). Placed creatures cannot be sold. Auto-fill searches for the highest-income four-part combination under the implemented formula, including mutation harmony; the server still validates the chosen parts.
- Twelve extra stands unlock one at a time with cash and rebirth milestones. First costs $2,500 at rebirth 1; each following stand doubles the previous price and requires one additional rebirth. Extra stands survive rebirth. Limited plot teleport buttons have a server cooldown.
- Five optional gamepass hooks are disabled by default. When configured and confirmed by Roblox server-side ownership checks, Luck doubles non-common tier weights, AutoRoll unlocks auto-roll before rebirth 5, KeepSlots retains roll slots on rebirth, Backpack raises the body-part cap from 100 to 200, and VIP adds 20% online stand income. Luck stacks multiplicatively with event weights and VIP stacks with event income multipliers. VIP chat/booth cosmetics and VIP offline bonuses are not implemented. Developer products remain disabled. Ownership-query failure is treated as unknown; a potentially destructive rebirth is blocked if KeepSlots has not been confirmed. A confirmed KeepSlots entitlement is also retained in the saved profile to protect slots against later configuration/service failures. Never configure paid features for public sale until Roblox tests pass; see `docs/GAMEPASS_SETUP.md`.
- A separate 15-booth showcase hub can be configured with two place IDs; players claim a booth, display up to four owned creatures and inspect displays. The hub loads the same locked profile without changing its inventory. Booths are temporary; return and entry portals require published places in the same experience. **There are no transactions, prices or item transfers yet.** See `docs/TRADING_HUB_SETUP.md`.
- Saved profiles are checked for basic schema and inventory consistency before loading; invalid data is not replaced. The bundled mock test covers basic lock exclusion and stale-owner writes, not actual Roblox behavior.
- The current luck model multiplies rarer tier weights increasingly with 1.15 compound rebirth luck; it is not a guarantee of a particular rarity. Probabilities depend on unlocked tiers. Slots and HUD collection are usable from anywhere. Offer text shows the tier chance at roll time, not the probability of a specific archetype, body part or mutation.

## Next: quality and full feature coverage
Add robust device testing, more elaborate tutorial arrows and character effects, licensed audio assets (the configurable hooks are silent by default), richer animations, procedural character detailing, additional event presentation, randomized disasters, polish and accessibility. Define the exact EXP progression and luck formula if different from the above. Review Roblox policies and third-party names/assets.

## Later: trading and monetization
Extend the showcase hub with recoverable, secure transfers, booth sales and direct trades; test race conditions, session ownership and recovery extensively. Roblox DataStores do not provide transactions across two player profiles. Configure place IDs, player limits, product IDs and prices in Creator Dashboard. Implement idempotent server receipt handling before enabling purchases. Paid random items and their disclosures must comply with Roblox policy. Paid skips, missing VIP cosmetics/offline benefits and cash packs remain unimplemented; the limited admin panel is not a full moderation suite.

## Validation before release
Perform Studio and published test-place tests for persistence, lease expiry, server crashes, overlapping sessions, rejoin and shutdown. Test malformed remote inputs, concurrent actions and client disconnections. Validate balance and responsive UI on real mobile and PC devices. Test real paid receipts and trading recovery only in an appropriate test environment. Do not treat static syntax/config checks as Roblox runtime validation.
