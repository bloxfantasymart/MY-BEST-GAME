# Testing the development build

These steps are for a **private test experience**, not your public game. The automatic checks in the README do not run Roblox Studio or prove that the game is safe to release.

## 1. Start the game

1. Follow the Rojo connection steps in the README. In Roblox Studio, use **View → Output** to see errors.
2. Click **Play**. Your character should appear near your plot. Check that the plot has a nameplate, three stations and 24 stands; the first 12 should be yellow and the rest gray.
3. Check the tutorial goal and station highlight. Walk to the red station and use its prompt. Pull the lever; check that a tray shows an offer. Buy it or press its Skip button. Try walking away and trying to roll: the server should reject the action.
4. Keep rolling and buying until you have a Head, Chest, Arms and Legs. Go to the purple pod. Select each part (or use auto-fill), then assemble. Verify the parts disappear and the creature appears in the backpack.
5. Use a yellow stand prompt and select the creature. It should appear, move gently and earn pending cash. Step on the green pad or tap the cash counter to collect. Check the displayed balance and level increase over time. Verify tutorial completion grants $1,000 once, stays complete after rejoining, and does not pay again.
6. Open **UPGRADES → PLOT THEMES** and select an unlocked theme; locked themes must not apply. Check the title above your avatar after respawning. Test inventory filtering, selling confirmation, paid leveling, travel and upgrades. Selling is permanent. Auto-roll requires rebirth 5 or the configured pass. Test it only after reading its warning: it spends cash. Move away from the station and check it stops.
7. Open **REWARDS → HOW TO PLAY**, check the two plaza leaderboards and the event countdown. The leaderboards cover the current server only. Use Studio's device emulator and a real mobile device to check that buttons and text remain usable.

### Season crate and capacity milestones

**Update and publish both main and hub places, then shut down all old servers before testing.** Older builds reject inventories above 250 parts; use a separate private test experience and back up test data. Check that an older profile with claimed tier 10, 30 or 50 receives the corresponding +5 slots per milestone without receiving extra old cash/vouchers or retroactive crates. Claims remain sequential; preview every page and verify the server's next reward matches the UI. Claim each milestone and check capacity after rejoin, rebirth and hub travel. With all cash upgrades, season slots and a verified test Backpack pass, fill 265 parts; test gift/roll/crate capacity rejection and bulk sale. When pass ownership is unavailable, preserve already-owned extra parts.

For unclaimed tier 6 and other non-milestone multiples of six, claim a free Standard Roll Crate. A full (100) crate inventory must block the claim without advancing the tier. Open crates from Rewards and Backpack; verify 80% Common / 20% Uncommon tier odds, uniform archetype selection within each tier, 25% per part type and Standard mutation, independent of luck/rebirth. With no space, failed generation, duplicate ID, repeat and forged requests, verify no crate or other item is lost. Statistical checks and the standalone mocks do not substitute for Roblox networking, DataStore, economy or GUI testing. Paid rewards remain disabled.

### Elapsed online income and level timing

In a private Studio server, place a creature with a known base rate, record the bank, wait a measured interval and compare the accrued value and level progression. Include fractional seconds around a level boundary, two adjacent creatures with an ascension bonus, and manual collection. Place and remove a creature between ticks, upgrade a placed creature, rebirth-sacrifice a placed creature, unlock the Sigma Statue, change VIP status in an appropriate test environment and leave/shut down; the previous rate should be settled before the change or save. Test the auto-collector and visit the hub. If deliberately simulating lag, verify short delays catch up, but a single checkpoint discards elapsed time beyond 30 seconds. Scheduled event boundaries between checkpoints are approximate, so test near Sigma/weekend transitions and admin event overrides without expecting exact sub-second payouts. Offline earnings still use a separate 25% calculation and do not grant EXP.

The standalone elapsed-time tests check formulas and capped delays, not Roblox server scheduling, exact event-boundary timing, DataStores or device performance. Verify with a separate published test experience before using real data.

### Showcase inventory and inspection

In the published private test experience, use the same experience's main and hub places with two accounts. Bring an inventory of more than 12 creatures, including similarly named creatures with different components, mutations, levels, protection, ascension and blueprint flags. Claim a booth, search by name and component with mixed case and literal punctuation, try each sort, navigate pages and hide/show already displayed creatures. Add up to four; check that a fifth or duplicate is refused by the server, and that protected and placed creatures can be displayed without changing ownership. Remove a display and review, cancel, then confirm booth release; your saved inventory must remain unchanged.

From the second account inspect the displayed component descriptions, rarity, blueprint/ascension and standalone income. Confirm the rest of the owner's inventory is not included in the visitor payload and that another player cannot add, remove or release the booth with forged requests. Change the displays and use Refresh from the visitor. Try leaving range, closing a panel before a delayed response arrives, switching booths or opening accessibility while a response is pending, typing during refresh, rotating/resizing, leaving/rejoining and hub travel. These paths require Roblox multiplayer and device tests; the standalone view and booth tests do not simulate GUI input, networking or teleports. There are no purchases or trades.

### Persistence ordering and malformed profiles

Use a separate private published test experience with Studio API access enabled only for that experience. With test-only instrumentation or a controlled DataStore wrapper, delay one autosave and enqueue two later snapshots; resume the newer waiter before the older one and verify the older snapshot cannot overwrite it. Repeat with a departure or shutdown while saves are queued: the final snapshot should supersede waiting autosaves and retain its inventory even if the live tables change after it was queued. Simulate failed writes, a lost lease, timeouts and shutdowns; these guards cannot guarantee a successful final save.

Inject malformed data only into disposable test keys: sparse/dictionary-shaped part and creature inventories, empty IDs and invalid lease fields must fail to load and leave the stored profile untouched. Existing valid empty and populated inventories should still load. Do not test by modifying real player data; there is no automatic repair tool, so preserve backups and investigate a rejected profile before any manual intervention. The standalone tests simulate concurrency with coroutines, not Roblox DataStore scheduling or outages.

### Daily quest item rewards and free mystery boxes

In a private test place, complete all three daily objectives and check their displayed cash/item rewards and 50/75/100 season XP. Claim each twice and send forged quest keys; only the first valid claim pays. Set a test profile's 2x-potion inventory to 100 and its box inventory to 100, complete the corresponding quests and verify the claims remain available without losing their rewards until space is made (or UTC midnight resets the quests). Test 2x potion activation, extension of the existing 2x timer, stacking with 3x weight boosts, save/rejoin and hub travel.

Open earned boxes from Rewards and Backpack → Boosts: every box should grant one Standard Uncommon body part, potentially above the player's rebirth unlock. All seven Uncommon archetypes and all four part types are uniform and independent of luck; verify the disclosure. With a full backpack, repeated/forged requests, a simulated generation failure or ID collision, verify the box is not consumed and no partial reward is added. Test legacy profiles with missing new balances, round trips, invalid saved balances and clients on both places. The standalone tests use mocks, not Roblox DataStores, GUI or real server concurrency. Update and publish both places together and shut down old servers before testing; use a separate test experience.

### Large-inventory crafting, fusion and stands

In Studio, fill the part inventory (up to 265) with multiple kinds, rarities, mutations, protected items and duplicate names. Open the pod, switch all four sockets, search by name and mutation (including literal punctuation), sort and change pages. Verify selections persist across pages and are shown in the socket summary; clear and reselect, use Auto-Fill, and toggle the blueprint filter. Check the preview and confirmation against the actual output. Protect or remove a selected item before confirming and verify the server rejects stale or invalid selections without consuming anything.

At rebirth 15+, prepare matching and mismatched creatures, including protected, placed, low-level, ascended and blueprint variants. Search and page the forge; after selecting one, only eligible matching recipes should appear. Remove individual selections, clear all, cancel confirmation and complete a valid fusion. Verify the server still rejects manipulated IDs and changed inputs atomically. At a stand, search/sort/page unplaced creatures (including protected ones), place one, then remove it from the occupant row; a creature on another stand cannot be selected until removed there. Repeat with viewport resizing and while text fields are focused. Standalone picker and recipe tests do not exercise actual Roblox GUI input or networking.

### State refresh and shutdown departures

In the main place, open **Rewards → Refresh game state from server** and confirm the displayed inventory and money match the server. Try while a search or auto-roll input is focused and as creatures level or an odds response arrives; typing should remain intact and the menu should update after focus is released. Check initial loading and periodic refresh with simulated latency. The refresh is a throttled RemoteEvent request, not a guarantee against a disconnected client.

With the separate private persistence-enabled test experience, arrange for a player to leave while a save is delayed, then shut down the same server. Check the Output logs and verify the departure save is included in the shutdown wait; repeat for both main and hub, including concurrent joins and autosaves. Test the 25-second timeout and service failure as well: shutdown and final saves are still best-effort, and progress can be lost. With a configured test Backpack pass, ten cash upgrades and all three season milestones, fill all 265 part slots; confirm a full bulk sale succeeds, protected parts are excluded by the UI, and stale/protected/malformed selections fail atomically. The standalone gate and inventory tests do not simulate the Roblox scheduler or real DataStores.

### Responsive layout and accessibility

Use Studio's device emulator and real phones/tablets in both portrait and landscape, plus PC. Check the HUD, right-side navigation, long text, close buttons, scrolling lists, text inputs and all panels before and after resizing or rotating. Check the top bar and device notches/safe areas and ensure the bottom-left movement controls remain usable. In the main place open **REWARDS → ACCESSIBILITY SETTINGS**; turn reduced motion, sound muting and event tint hiding on and off while displays, mutations, events and permitted audio are active. Check that display idle, rainbow cycling and Fire/Sparkles stop locally, resume when enabled, and do not alter another player's view or any gameplay. Test effects streamed in while motion is reduced, leaving/returning to the 100-stud range, display removal and respawn. In the hub, use **ACCESSIBILITY** to test reduced motion there. Verify settings reset on rejoin and place changes. The pure sizing and mocked animation tests do not verify actual safe-area behavior, input, rendering or performance.

### Creature visual identity and level changes

In a private test place, assemble several creatures with different Head, Chest, Arms and Legs archetypes and mutations. Place them on stands and verify their procedural headwear, face, chest detail, hands and feet reflect the corresponding components. Check the rarity-colored label, level and stand income. Level a placed creature through levels 4→5 and 9→10: eyes should become neon at level 5 and a rarity-colored halo should appear at level 10, without duplication or loss of income. Check ascended crown, mutation effects, save/rejoin, changing stands, plot cleanup and the showcase hub. Cosmetic changes must not affect income or inventory.

Repeat with streaming enabled, move across the 100-stud animation/effect threshold, and test multiple players on mobile and PC. Fill as many stands and hub booths as practical while monitoring memory, frame rate and network usage; extra geometric detail increases part count and distant geometry is not culled by this LOD. The bundled structural test uses Roblox API stubs and cannot check rendering, collision, streaming or live performance.

### Explicit rebirth review

In a private test profile, prepare several matching target creatures with different levels, mutations, blueprint status and stand placement. Open **REBIRTH**, select one and request review. Verify the server review shows the exact chosen creature, components, total wallet cash to be reset, Keep Slots result and unpurchased offers. Cancel once and verify nothing changes; then confirm and check only the chosen creature is sacrificed, all wallet cash resets, pending bank and other creatures remain, and a sacrificed placed creature is removed from its stand. Check both confirmed Keep Slots and disabled/not-owned pass scenarios in a suitable private environment.

Try protected, ascended, wrong-target, missing and another player's creatures; they must be rejected. Test insufficient cash, a running auto-roll, and max rebirth. After review, change cash, level, placement, selected protection, slots, offers or pass ownership, or wait 30 seconds: confirmation should be rejected without consuming anything. Try a wrong token, repeated/replayed confirmation and confirmation without review. If auto-collection changes wallet cash while reviewing, request a fresh review. These checks still need real multiplayer and DataStore testing.

### Assembly review and safety

At the pod, select four different owned parts. Verify the preview name follows the first highest-rarity part in Head, Chest, Arms, Legs order, and the level-1 rate matches the actual creature. Try matching mutations and mixed mutations, with and without a rare blueprint, and check the harmony and blueprint multipliers. Cancel and change selection before confirming; no items should be consumed. Confirm a valid recipe and verify exactly four parts become one creature, with the tutorial, daily quest and season XP advancing correctly. Try stale selections, protected or missing parts, duplicates, a full creature inventory, insufficient blueprints and forged remote requests; all failures should leave items and blueprint counts unchanged. Check the forge's selected component details and output preview against the actual result. Test with two players and on mobile; the preview does not replace server validation.

### Fusion and neighboring stands

In a private test profile at rebirth 15 or above, prepare three distinct, unplaced, non-ascended creatures with the same four components and mutations, each at level 10 or higher. At the plaza forge, check that too little cash, different mutations, a placed creature, repeated selections and rebirth below 15 cannot consume anything. Confirm a valid fusion; verify exactly three inputs and the fee disappear, and one level-1 ascended creature with 5x base income appears. Place it next to another creature: the adjacent stand's displayed rate should rise by 20%. Test corners, row edges, multiple ascended neighbors and removal. Save/rejoin and visit the hub to check the flag and appearance survive. Also verify offline income includes adjacency once. These behaviors need real Studio and published-environment testing, not just the pure-logic checks.

### Session gifts and roll expiry

In a private server, open **REWARDS** and try claiming each session gift early; it must not grant anything. After one minute, claim the cash gift twice and verify only the first claim pays. After five minutes, claim the Uncommon part with space available; repeat with a full inventory and ensure it remains claimable after freeing space. At 15 minutes, claim the timed luck boost, verify the countdown and boosted tier odds, then rejoin or visit the hub and check the boost continues until its saved expiry. Rejoining resets the session gift timer and allows gifts to be earned again. At 3 minutes, test vouchers as described below; at 60 minutes test the Epic+ gift with a full bag and with space; at 90 minutes claim the crown, respawn, rejoin and visit the hub. A duplicate crown pays $100,000. At 25 minutes claim the rare blueprint and test it as described below. Three unspecified gift tiers remain unimplemented.

Roll a part and leave the offer unpurchased for more than 60 seconds. Its tray should clear and a late buy request must not deduct cash or grant the item. Check that auto-roll still refreshes its own offers. At the roll station, use a voucher on an offer and verify no cash is deducted; retry the same offer, move away, fill inventory, and wait for expiry to check no voucher is consumed. Check auto-roll still uses cash. Compare auto-fill's selected income against different mixed and same-mutation combinations.

### Daily rewards and objectives

Open **REWARDS**, claim the daily reward twice, and check that only the first claim pays. With controlled test profiles, check all seven streak days, potion/voucher caps, duplicate unlock cash, 3x potion countdown and stacking, Golden Lever odds, and Sigma Statue income online and offline. Verify the unlock visuals, save/rejoin, hub round trip and plot cleanup. Verify the 60-minute gift tier distribution against the shown weights. Roll 50 parts (multi-slot rolls count each part), then claim the roll objective once. Complete the other objectives and verify each can only pay once. UTC midnight resets the objectives. Verify a missed UTC day resets the login streak; Studio clock changes alone should not be trusted as a server-time test.

### Catalog and roll odds

From **REWARDS → CATALOG & ROLL ODDS** or the roll station, browse all 50 entries, search by name, change rarity and unlocked-only filters, and test both page arrows. At several rebirths check locked/unlocked entries, current tier percentages, unlocked archetype counts, base prices and uniform per-archetype chances. Refresh during Frenzy, weekend, and timed luck boosts, and (only in a private pass-test experience) after verifying the Luck pass. Golden Lever changes non-Common weights by 1.05; it does not multiply the displayed probability by 1.05. Compare the server snapshot with the tier probability printed on newly rolled offers, allowing for event or boost changes between requests. Verify mutation percentages total 100% and each part type is 25%; session gift odds use a different calculation. Snapshots are timestamped and can become stale: refresh after rebirth or modifier changes. Test on mobile as well as PC. Automated statistical sampling is not a substitute for Roblox testing.

### Blueprint and backpack upgrades

Claim the 25-minute blueprint in a private server. At the assembly pod, toggle it on and check that Auto-Fill selects only unprotected Rare+ parts. Try a missing, protected, duplicate, Common or Uncommon part, no blueprint, a full creature inventory and a forged non-boolean setting: none should consume the blueprint or any parts. Confirm a valid four-Rare+ assembly and compare its base income with the same recipe without a blueprint: it should be 25% higher, in addition to any matching-mutation bonus. Check it persists after rejoin and the hub, earns income on a stand, and can fuse only with matching blueprint-boosted creatures. Test the blueprint cap without consuming an unclaimable gift.

Buy backpack upgrades under **UPGRADES** with sufficient and insufficient cash. Each should charge once and add five part slots, up to ten upgrades; check the maximum, save/rejoin, rebirth, auto-roll, gift claims and hub round trip. Season tiers 10, 30 and 50 add 15 slots in total; a verified Backpack pass adds another 100, up to 265 total; do not enable public sales. Test a temporary entitlement failure with extra items present: existing items must remain, but capacity can be reduced until ownership is verified. **Update and publish both places and shut down old servers first**, because older servers may reject or discard the new fields or expanded inventory.

### Auto-roll safety and per-rarity rules

In a private test profile at rebirth 5, start with no current offers and open **CONFIGURE AUTO-ROLL**. Verify the defaults skip Common/Uncommon and hold all higher tiers, and that starting with existing offers is refused until you buy, skip or confirm discarding them. Check Skip removes only unpurchased offers; Hold stops and leaves that and later slots for review until their normal 60-second expiration. Configure Buy on several tiers, enter low total and per-part cash limits and a small batch limit, review and confirm; ensure slot order, amounts spent, counters and stopping reason are correct. Test exact budget/price boundaries, insufficient cash, full inventory, multiple slots, a held item after earlier purchases, manual-buy requests during a run, stopping, moving away, entitlement loss and repeated starts. Test malformed/forged settings, missing rules, fractional or negative limits and an oversized batch count; none should start a run. Auto-roll uses cash, not vouchers; manually buying after stopping is outside the run budget. Leave/rejoin and confirm it never starts automatically. Repeat with two players near different stations. Use small test amounts: sales and purchases are real profile changes.

### Auto-collector and income breakdown

In a private test experience, open **UPGRADES** below rebirth 10 and verify the auto-collector cannot be enabled, including by a forged request. At rebirth 10, turn it on and place a creature. Verify whole dollars transfer approximately every 10 seconds, fractional pending cash remains, an empty bank is not repeatedly collected, and daily ATM collection progress advances exactly once. Test the tutorial, manual collection just before the timer, rapid toggles, leaving/rejoining, travelling through the hub, and a failed profile load. While offline or in the hub there is no auto-collection; normal offline earnings still apply on main-place load. Disable the drone and confirm collection stops and its placeholder visual disappears. If using the private admin tool, reduce rebirth below 10 and check it switches off. Make sure a departing player's drone does not appear for the next plot owner.

Open **UPGRADES → PLOT INCOME BREAKDOWN**, compare the displayed rates with placed creatures, level changes and ascension neighbors, then check the Sigma Statue and available events. Test VIP only in an appropriate private pass-testing environment. The online estimate should reflect live bonuses; offline excludes VIP and events, includes the statue and caps at 24 hours. Verify the display on mobile and check actual collection under server load: rates are estimates and delays can lower real online income.

### Backpack safety and browsing

In a private test profile, obtain several parts and creatures. Open **BACKPACK** and test all four tabs, literal name/type/mutation search, each protection filter and sort order, and both page arrows with more than 20 matching items. Select an item and protect it, then try individual sale, bulk sale, assembly/autofill, fusion and rebirth sacrifice as applicable. None may consume a protected item, even with forged or stale requests; verify an attempted mixed bulk sale cancels completely. A protected creature should still be placeable and levelable. Unprotect and repeat a sale or craft deliberately. Bulk sale ignores browsing filters and covers all unprotected parts up to the selected rarity; review the count and value and cancel once before confirming. Check protection after save/rejoin and hub visits, and test on mobile as well as PC. Always update both places and shut down old servers before testing new saved fields.

### Free season track and repeatable rolls

In a private test place, open **REWARDS → OPEN FREE SEASON TRACK**. Roll parts with one and multiple slots and check +1 XP and one objective count per generated part, including unpurchased offers and auto-roll. Assemble a creature (+20 XP), rebirth (+100 XP), and claim each daily objective (50/75/100 XP, only once). Verify a tier cannot be claimed early, earned tiers are claimed in order exactly once, cash/potions/vouchers/crates/slot milestones match the shown reward, and full potion, voucher or crate inventories prevent the relevant claim without consuming it. Roll at least 500 parts and claim the repeatable objective twice, verifying excess carries over and the voucher cap blocks a claim without losing progress. Save/rejoin and visit the hub to check XP, claims and progress survive; old profiles should start at zero. Also test two clients and forged/repeated requests. This permanent prototype has no premium purchase or season reset.

## 2. Test multiple players

In Studio's **Test** tab start a server with two or more clients. Each player should get a different plot. A player must not be able to buy from another player's roll station, place creatures on another plot, or collect another plot's bank. Check that leaderboards update. Studio's default test profiles are temporary, even after a rejoin.

## 3. Test real saving carefully

1. Publish a **separate private test experience**, then follow the README's instructions to enable Studio API access and `DataService.TestPersistence`. Studio can modify real DataStore data for that experience.
2. Earn cash, buy parts, craft and place a creature, then stop and rejoin. Check cash, inventory, levels and placements. Leave a creature on a stand, leave for a few minutes, then return and verify the 25% capped offline amount appears in pending cash only once. Test entering and returning from the hub too. Test upgraded stands when you've earned them. Do not assume a single successful test proves saving is reliable.
3. Try a second session on the same account: it must not load while the first session holds its lease. If a server crashes, the lease can take up to 180 seconds to expire. A failed load should not create a fresh profile.
4. Test server shutdown while joins are in flight in both places, duplicate join attempts, disconnect during saves, copy/validation errors, service errors, and retry/rejoin behavior in a controlled private environment. Verify new joins do not start once shutdown begins, late successful loads are released, shutdown waits for in-flight joins up to its 25-second deadline, and invalid snapshots never overwrite valid data. Check Output for warnings. An unsuccessful final save can still lose progress.
5. Restore `DataService.TestPersistence = false` after testing in Studio. Published servers use the live DataStore regardless of this Studio-only switch.

## 4. Optional admin and performance tests

Follow [ADMIN_SETUP.md](ADMIN_SETUP.md) in a private test experience. Confirm an unauthorized account cannot open the panel or execute forged remote requests, and check numeric boundaries, confirmations, the selected target, event expiry and save/rejoin. Admin actions affect real profile data. Test display motion and mutation effects at distances below and above 100 studs with multiple players; repeat with streaming enabled and check that models load correctly. This lightweight LOD has not been validated on devices.

## 5. Before publishing broadly

The optional [showcase hub](TRADING_HUB_SETUP.md) is not a marketplace yet. Trading and developer products are not implemented. Optional gamepass hooks are disabled by default; see the [gamepass guide](GAMEPASS_SETUP.md) and do not enable public sales before testing. Complete and test them separately before charging players. Review third-party character names, licensed assets and Roblox policies. Check performance with a full six-player server, including many placed creatures and events. Event timings are based on UTC, so servers should agree when their clocks agree.

## Deploying schema changes

Update and publish both the main place and the hub when scripts or profile fields change. Existing servers can continue running older code after publishing; shut down old private test servers before testing new profile fields, or an old server could save without them. Do not rely on production data until save/rejoin and cross-place compatibility have been verified.
