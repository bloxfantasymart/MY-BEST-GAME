=== 1. GAME OVERVIEW & CORE LOOP ===
Game Title: Roll a Brainrot
Primary Loop:
1. Pull the Slot Lever on your plot to roll body parts (Head, Chest, Arms, Legs).
2. Choose to BUY rolled parts for cash or SKIP/REROLL them by pulling again.
3. Bring 4 matching or mismatched parts to the Assembly Pod to craft a custom Brainrot.
4. Place crafted Brainrots on plot stands to generate passive $/second.
5. Upgrade Brainrot levels to multiply passive income and collect accumulated cash.
6. Rebirth up to 100 times by meeting cash requirements and sacrificing specific Brainrots.
7. Use the Central Portal to visit the 15-player Trading Hub to sell Brainrots at custom player booths.

Server Capacities:
- Standard Gameplay Server: 6 Players maximum (ensures smooth plot rendering and zero lag).
- Trading Hub Server: 15 Players maximum.

=== 4. LEVER ROLLING & MULTI-SLOT SYSTEM ===
1. Lever Interaction:
   - Pulling the lever activates roll animations across all unlocked slots.
   - Sound FX: Satisfying clicky mechanical ratchet sound on pull, fanfare chime on rare drops.
2. Slot Upgrades (Max 9 Slots):
   - Slot 1: Free (Default)
   - Slot 2: $500
   - Slot 3: $2,500
   - Slot 4: $10,000
   - Slot 5: $50,000
   - Slot 6: $250,000
   - Slot 7: $1,000,000
   - Slot 8: $5,000,000
   - Slot 9: $25,000,000
3. Roll Resolution:
   - When pulled, each active slot rolls an independent body part (Head, Chest, Arms, or Legs) based on player's Rebirth luck multiplier.
   - Above each slot pad, a floating 3D part preview appears with a billboard showing:
     * Part Name (e.g., "Skibidi Toilet Head")
     * Rarity Badge (e.g., "[Rare 1 in 15]")
     * Purchase Price
   - Two buttons per slot: [BUY] or [SKIP / REROLL].
   - Buying deducts cash and deposits the piece into the player's Backpack.
   - Pulling the lever again automatically discards unpurchased parts and rolls a fresh batch.

=== 5. ASSEMBLY MACHINE POD (CRAFTING) ===
1. Mechanics:
   - Player approaches the Pod and opens the Assembly GUI.
   - UI features 4 circular equipment sockets: [HEAD], [CHEST], [ARMS], [LEGS].
   - Players select 1 owned piece for each socket from their inventory. Parts can belong to completely different Brainrot archetypes (e.g., Dirt Boy Head + Gigachad Prime Chest + Skibidi Toilet Arms + Mewing Chad Legs).
2. Synthesis Output:
   - Income Calculation: Sum of all 4 parts' base income multiplied by the highest rarity component multiplier:
     Income/Sec = (HeadIncome + ChestIncome + ArmsIncome + LegsIncome) * (HighestComponentMultiplier * 0.4)
   - Name Generation: "{Highest Rarity Part Name} Abomination"
   - Model Assembly: Procedurally attaches the selected Head, Arms, and Legs onto the Torso root part using Motor6D / WeldConstraints.
3. Level Progression:
   - Crafted Brainrots start at Level 1.
   - Earn 1 EXP per 10 seconds placed on a stand, or can be instantly leveled with cash.
   - Each level increases $/sec income by +25%.

=== 6. TYCOON INCOME & STANDS ===
1. Plot Placement:
   - 12 total display stands per plot.
   - Approaching an empty stand prompts player to select an assembled Brainrot from inventory.
   - Display stand mounts the composite 3D character with an idle breathing animation.
2. Server Income Tick:
   - Server runs a synchronized 1-second heartbeat loop.
   - Calculates total $/sec across all 12 stands for each player and adds to their `PendingCash` balance.
3. ATM Cash Station:
   - Floating green 3D numbers above the plot bank display accumulated pending money.
   - Stepping on the collection pad or pressing the HUD button immediately transfers pending money to the player's main balance with a coin-shower particle effect.

=== 7. 100-TIER REBIRTH SYSTEM ===
1. Rebirth Scaling:
   - Maximum Rebirth Cap: 100.
   - Cash Formula: math.floor(1000 * (RebirthTier + 1)^2.7)
   - Sacrifice Requirement: Must own and sacrifice 1 specific assembled Brainrot matching the Rebirth tier bracket (e.g., Rebirth 1 requires "Dirt Boy Abomination", Rebirth 5 requires "Quandale Dingle Abomination").
2. Standard Rebirth Reset Rules:
   - Cash resets to $0.
   - Roll slots reset back to 1 (unless player owns the Infinite Slots gamepass).
   - The sacrificed Brainrot is permanently removed from inventory.
   - Placed Brainrots, Plot Stands, and all other inventory items DO NOT reset.
3. Rebirth Rewards:
   - Permanent Luck Boost: +15% compound luck per rebirth level.
   - Unlocks higher catalog archetypes for lever rolling.
4. Robux Skip Option:
   - Players can bypass requirements with Robux (starts at 49 Robux, increases +15 Robux per Rebirth level).
   - Skipping with Robux does NOT wipe cash and does NOT consume any Brainrots.

=== 8. TRADING HUB & BOOTH SYSTEM ===
1. Architecture:
   - Main world portal teleports players to a dedicated 15-player trading place using `TeleportService`.
2. Booth Assignment:
   - 15 numbered booths around a town square.
   - Interacting with an unclaimed booth claims it for the player and displays their username and avatar thumbnail.
3. Selling & Buying:
   - Owner can list up to 4 assembled Brainrots on their booth counter.
   - Owner sets custom cash prices for each listing.
   - Other players walk up, inspect the 4 combined parts and level stats via a 3D viewport, and click "Buy".
   - Server verifies the buyer's balance, deducts cash, transfers ownership of the Brainrot, and awards cash to the seller instantly.
4. Direct Trading Window:
   - Players can click another player to send a trade request.
   - Standard 2-way security trade window with a mandatory 5-second lock confirmation countdown to prevent swap scams.

=== 11. ANTICHEAT & SECURITY ===
1. Server Authoritative Architecture:
   - Clients NEVER tell the server how much money they have, what parts they rolled, or what they crafted.
   - Clients only send intent requests: `RequestRoll()`, `PurchasePart(partId)`, `Assemble(partIds)`, `CollectCash()`.
2. Exploitation Safeguards:
   - Proximity Validation: Player character must be within 20 studs of the Lever or Pod to invoke action remotes.
   - Remote Throttling / Rate-Limiting: Enforce a strict 0.35-second cooldown on rolling to prevent automated exploit spamming.
   - Numerical Sanitization: Reject `NaN`, `math.huge`, negative numbers, or non-integer inputs on all transactions.
   - Inventory Ownership Checks: Verify that every part ID submitted for crafting exists in the player's server-stored inventory before destroying or merging.

## 5. CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

### A. Lever Rolling Station & Slot Scaling
- **Physical Lever:** Pulling triggers mechanical ratcheting sounds, arm rotation tweens, and visual drops on 1 to 9 active drop pads.
- **Slot Upgrades (In-Game Cash):**
  - Slot 1: Free (Default)
  - Slot 2: $500
  - Slot 3: $2,500
  - Slot 4: $10,000
  - Slot 5: $50,000
  - Slot 6: $250,000
  - Slot 7: $1,000,000
  - Slot 8: $5,000,000
  - Slot 9: $25,000,000
- **Buy / Skip Mechanics:** Each rolled pad displays a 3D preview, name, rarity, and buy price. Players can [BUY] to deposit into their backpack or pull the lever again to discard and reroll all slots.
- **Auto-Roll:** Features automated rolling and customizable auto-skip/auto-buy rarity filters.

### B. The 4-Part Assembly Machine Pod
- Approaching the pod opens a 4-socket UI: `[Head]`, `[Chest]`, `[Arms]`, `[Legs]`.
- Players can mix and match parts from any archetypes in their backpack.
- **Synthesis:** Server verifies ownership, burns the 4 parts, welds the physical models together, and calculates composite stats:
  $$\text{Income/Sec} = \sum(\text{Part Base Income}) \times (\text{Highest Rarity Multiplier} \times 0.4) \times \text{Mutation Bonuses}$$
- Resulting creature is named: `"{Dominant Part} Abomination"`.

### C. Tycoon Plot & Passive Income Engine
- **Stands:** Base plot includes 12 stands (expandable up to 24 via cash and rebirth milestones).
- **Passive Tick (1 Hz):** Server computes combined $/sec across all stands and routes earnings into the plot's `PendingCash` bank.
- **ATM Collection:** Stepping on the plot ATM or clicking the collect button claims accumulated money with particle explosions and register audio.
- **Leveling:** Placed Brainrots accumulate passive EXP or can be leveled up using cash, granting +25% income per level.

### D. 100-Tier Rebirth System
- **Scale:** Caps at Rebirth 100.
- **Cash Requirement:** `math.floor(1000 * (RebirthTier + 1)^2.7)`
- **Sacrifice Requirement:** Requires consuming 1 specific assembled Brainrot matching the tier bracket.
- **Standard Reset:** Wipes cash to $0 and resets roll slots to 1 (unless the player owns the Keep Slots pass). Placed Brainrots, stands, and inventory items remain untouched.
- **Reward:** Permanent +15% compound luck multiplier per rebirth and unlocks higher catalog roll tiers.
- **Robux Skip:** Bypasses cash and sacrifice requirements without wiping cash. Price scales with tier level.

### E. Dedicated Trading Hub & Booths (15 Players)
- Accessible via the central world portal through `TeleportService`.
- 15 interactable merchant stalls where players list up to 4 assembled Brainrots with custom cash price tags.
- Direct 2-way trade windows featuring an item lock state and a 5-second anti-scam countdown timer.
- Economy sink: 5% marketplace transaction fee applied on booth purchases.

### F. Dynamic Live Events & Weather
- **Timed Luck Cycle (Every 30 mins):** Triggers a 5-minute "Brainrot Frenzy" awarding 2x global roll luck with ambient neon skybox shifts.
- **Random Disasters (Every 15–20 mins):**
  - "Ohio Storm": Golden rain falls; touching drops grants instant bonus cash.
  - "Skibidi Eclipse": 50% discount on all roll purchase prices for 3 minutes.
  - "Sigma Awakening": 3x passive plot income multiplier for 2 minutes.

## 7. SERVER SECURITY & ANTI-CHEAT ARCHITECTURE

- **Strict Server Authority:** Clients never dictate cash, drop results, or crafting outcomes. The client sends intents; the server computes, verifies, and commits all state.
- **Input Sanitization:** Rejects `NaN`, `math.huge`, negative numbers, strings where numbers are expected, and malformed payload tables.
- **Rate-Limiting & Debouncing:** Enforces a hard 0.35s roll debounce and throttles remote spam by dropping abusive requests for 5 seconds.
- **Spatial Validation:** Player character must be within 20 studs of the Lever, Pod, ATM, or Booth to trigger actions.
- **Transactional Persistence:** ProfileStore / DataStore session-locking architecture with automatic 300s saves and a 25-second `BindToClose` shutdown fail-safe.
